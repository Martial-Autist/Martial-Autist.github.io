Privacy Policy — Living Character Sheet

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The short version

Living Character Sheet does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your characters stay on your own device.

What the app stores

Everything you enter — characters, stats, spells, notes and any character portrait you add — is saved only in your device's local app storage. It is never sent to the developer or to any server. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, that information is deleted. Use the app's Export option to keep your own backup.

Accounts, analytics and ads

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics or tracking, and no advertising.

Optional tips

If you choose to leave a tip, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play (in the Android app) or Ko-fi (on the web). The developer never sees your payment details. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Network use

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts the first time it runs, and then works offline.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/Martial-Autist/Martial-Autist.github.io/issues.
