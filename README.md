# Living Character Sheet

A free, homebrew-friendly living character sheet for 5e.

**Use it:** https://martial-autist.github.io/

- Tap any skill, save, attack or spell to roll it
- Spell slots, upcasting, metamagic and class resources tracked for you
- Multiclassing and a guided level-up
- Add anything your DM gives you — items, features and custom options that change your numbers automatically
- Works offline; your characters stay on your device
